QUICK SUMMARY

We collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app. We do NOT collect your name, email, voice recordings, or any personally identifiable information. We do NOT sell your data.

1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to KawanBacaku. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we collect and use information.

By using KawanBacaku, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Automatically Collected Information:

  • Usage Information
    • Features you use, screens you view
    • Session duration and frequency
    • Story selection (titles and categories)
    • Recognition metrics (word count, accuracy)
    • Engine preference (online/offline mode)
  • Performance Data
    • App crashes and errors (anonymized)
    • Performance metrics (load times)
    • Technical diagnostics
  • Device Information
    • Device type (iOS/Android)
    • Operating system version
    • App version
    • Language preference
  • Anonymous Identifier
    • Randomly generated ID (NOT tied to your identity)
    • Can be reset by clearing app data
    • Used only for analytics

WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT

  • ✗ Your name, email, or contact information
  • ✗ Your speech transcripts or voice recordings
  • ✗ Audio recordings of your voice
  • ✗ Custom story content (only word count)
  • ✗ Device hardware IDs (IMEI, MAC address)
  • ✗ Precise geolocation
  • ✗ IP addresses
  • ✗ Any personally identifiable information (PII)

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use collected information to:

  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Understand which features are useful
  • Analyze usage patterns (anonymously)
  • Monitor errors and crashes
  • Enhance user experience

4. HOW WE SHARE INFORMATION

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Service Provider:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Crash Reporting)
  • Data shared is anonymized and encrypted
  • Privacy Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

We protect your data with:

  • ✓ Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • ✓ Encryption at rest on servers
  • ✓ Access controls and authentication
  • ✓ Anonymous identifiers only

Data Retention:

  • Analytics data: 14 months, then auto-deleted
  • Crash logs: 90 days
  • Local data: Until you delete the app

6. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Delete: Request deletion of your data
  • Object: Object to data processing
  • Portability: Get your data in portable format

How to Exercise Your Rights:

  1. Delete local data: Clear app data in device settings
  2. Delete server data: Contact us at gangkasa888@gmail.com

We will respond within 30 days.
